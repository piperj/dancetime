The Heats Tab, Under Pressure

A rehearsal for the five seconds you actually have, phone in hand, between heats.

2026-08-10 — two agents standing in for you: one walked the live site (piperj.github.io/dancetime/?competitor=Johan+Piper) on a mobile viewport with your real data, the other traced static/index.html's render logic directly.

Not a code review — a rehearsal. The brief was three questions, in your own words: when is it my turn? what dances am I doing? will I be surprised, alone on the floor?

The verdict, in one line

The underlying logic for "when's my turn" is already correct — it's just invisible until you go looking for it. That's the whole story, and it's a good one: nothing here needs new data or new computation, only a different default and a different visual treatment.

What's actually broken: the first five seconds

Open your own deep link right now, mid-competition, and here's what happens: you land at the top of your entire day — heat 414, 9:00am — not at "now." On a 42-heat day that's over 5,000 pixels of schedule between you and the thing you actually opened the app to see. The correct position exists and updates live (a marker that re-checks the real clock every 10 seconds and advances automatically), but reaching it today means noticing a small hamburger icon, tapping it, and tapping "Current heat" — a multi-step recovery from a bad default, exactly when you have the least patience for multi-step anything.

What's already good, so it doesn't get lost in the fix list

Dance names are legible on every card, no decoding required. The Contested badge, when it fires, is genuinely well done — yellow, a medal emoji for placements, tappable straight through to judges' scores. Font sizes are readable without zooming, tap targets are full-width, nothing forces horizontal scroll. The break/award markers interleaved into the schedule are a nice pacing touch. None of that needs to change.

The "alone on the floor" question — settled: leave it alone

This one got revisited mid-audit and an always-on binary-indicator redesign was drafted — then rejected on sight. The pills stay exactly as they are. No further work planned here without a fresh request.

  1. Auto-scroll to "now" on load. No more hunting for the hamburger menu — the scroll-to-marker code already exists for the manual tap, it just needs to fire on its own.
  2. A red "now" line, Apple Calendar-style, instead of a marker glued to one heat card — a horizontal line across the schedule at the actual current time, time label included, as sketched above.
  3. Slow, continuous auto-scroll to keep that line at a constant spot on screen (even while it's technically off-screen) — drifting at a steady rate rather than snapping every 10 seconds, and slowing further during a break so a quiet stretch doesn't scroll at the same pace as back-to-back heats.
  4. A floating play/pause button, replacing the hamburger-menu "Current heat" item entirely — toggles the slow auto-scroll on or off, and a double-tap re-centers the red line to the middle of the screen.

All four are now logged in CLAUDE.md's TODO list, in that order, since 1 and 2 are the prerequisites the other two build on.
